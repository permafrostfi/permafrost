Launch · upgrade key, once each

1. Set up the vault

—

Creates the vault, the PAIF token and the five token accounts. Once only. The name and symbol cannot be changed afterwards.

2. Seed the vault

—

Moves these amounts from your wallet into the vault and creates 25 PAIF that are locked forever. Once only. Every leg must be above zero; the amounts set the basket's starting weights.

3. Switch on bonus, fees and the admin key

—

Creates the bonus pool, the escrow for locked deposits and the fee account, and makes your wallet the admin key. Bonus and fees start off. Once only, and the last thing the upgrade key needs to do.

Admin powers · the complete list

Power 1 · Deposit cap

—

Deposits that would take total supply above the cap are refused. Redemptions are never capped.

Power 2 · Locked bonus

—

Depositors who lock their units for the lock period get this bonus from the pool. Changes apply to new locks only; open locks keep their terms.

Power 3 · Bonus pool

—

Anyone can top the pool up with PAIF from the connected wallet. Only the admin can withdraw, and only unused units: units promised to open locks are out of reach.

Power 4 · Fees

—

Caps: entry 1%, exit 1%, management 1% a year. A cut applies at once; a rise takes effect 30 days after you set it. Setting again replaces any pending change. Fees are paid as PAIF to your wallet's fixed fee account.

Power 5 · Hand over the admin key

—

Two steps. The admin names a new key; nothing changes until that key connects here and accepts. A typo can never hand the role to a key nobody holds.

Apply an upgrade

Claude uploads new program code to a buffer and hands the buffer to your address. Paste the buffer address here; the page checks it before you sign. The buffer's rent comes back to your wallet. The upgrade key is to be destroyed later, once v3 has run live for a while.
